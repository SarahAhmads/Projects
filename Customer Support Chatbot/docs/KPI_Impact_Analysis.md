# Business KPI Impact Analysis

Implementing the AI Customer Support Chatbot is expected to drive significant improvements across our primary customer service metrics.

## 1. Average Handling Time (AHT)
- **Current State:** Agents manually search through documentation to resolve technical queries.
- **AI Impact:** The RAG model surfaces the exact answer in milliseconds. Agents can either use the AI as an internal copilot or let the AI answer the customer directly.
- **Projected Improvement:** 25-30% reduction in AHT for standard inquiries.

## 2. First Contact Resolution (FCR)
- **Current State:** Complex issues are often escalated due to agents not finding the right documentation quickly.
- **AI Impact:** The vector search ensures the most relevant historical tickets and manuals are immediately available.
- **Projected Improvement:** 15% increase in FCR, reducing the need for follow-up calls or emails.

## 3. Customer Satisfaction Score (CSAT)
- **Current State:** Customers experience queue times during peak hours.
- **AI Impact:** The AI Chatbot offers instantaneous, 24/7 support without queueing.
- **Projected Improvement:** Expected 20% boost in overall CSAT due to zero wait times and accurate resolution.

## 4. Operational Cost Reduction
- **Impact:** By deflecting Tier-1 (repetitive) tickets, the system allows human agents to focus on high-value, complex cases, thereby optimizing workforce distribution and reducing cost per ticket.
